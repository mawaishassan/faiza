Frequently Asked Questions

What can I talk about?

You can bring up personal decisions, relationships, family matters, confidence, life changes, future plans or anything else within the scope of life coaching and personal development.

Do I need to prepare before the session?

No. You can simply arrive as you are. If it helps, think about one thing you would like to talk through, but there is no requirement to prepare.

Are sessions online or in-person?

Both options are available. The available format and location for an in-person meeting will be confirmed when arranging your appointment.

How long is a session and what does it cost?

Each session is 30 minutes and costs PKR 1,000.

How do I choose my appointment time?

First submit the booking form with your payment details. Once your payment is reviewed and approved, you will receive a scheduling link to select an available time.

Will what I share be kept private?

Your privacy will be treated with care. Before booking, please review the privacy and confidentiality terms, including any legal, safety or safeguarding limits. Do not use this service for emergencies.

Is this therapy or mental-health treatment?

No. This is a life-coaching and supportive listening service, not psychotherapy, diagnosis or medical treatment. If you need clinical or mental-health care, please contact a qualified mental-health professional.

Can a teenager book?

For anyone under 18, a parent or legal guardian must be involved and provide consent. The session will only proceed where appropriate safeguarding arrangements are in place.
